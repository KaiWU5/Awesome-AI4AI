A curated AI-for-AI research catalog

Awesome AI4AI tracks papers, benchmarks, implementations, and research releases about LLM agents and automated AI research. The collection is refreshed every week, with citation counts, GitHub stars, recent-paper rankings, primary-source links, and an auditable curation methodology.

The catalog covers agentic systems that plan and execute over long horizons, environments for measuring those systems, harnesses that shape their behavior, and methods that allow models or agents to improve AI workflows, code, data, training, and evaluation.

Open research resources

All catalog data is available as structured JSON in the data directory. The repository also provides an evidence audit, reproducible update scripts, dated weekly highlights, contribution guidance, and a machine-readable citation file.

Open Awesome AI4AI on GitHub to search the complete paper tables, star the project, suggest a paper, or contribute a correction.

Awesome AI4AI is maintained by Kai Wu, Hao Lyu, and Zhen Luo and released under the MIT License. The catalog accompanies the survey On the Eve of AI4AI: From Long-Horizon Agents to Recursive Self-Improvement.

## 5. NeoHorse-1: Towards Recursive Self-Improvement via Agentic Post-Training with Routing Harness

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08183)

- **Published:** 2026-09-08
- **Type / source:** Paper · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors log predicted capability demand, chosen service tier and interaction outcome for each turn, then reuse those routing signals as a three-stage fine-tuning curriculum and an evaluation-selection-update loop. They report macro-average gains from 58.94 to 64.87 at 4B and 65.60 to 69.04 at 9B across eleven benchmarks, with weights and code released.

## 6. EvoHarnessBench: Can Your Agents Keep Pace with an Evolving Harness?

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04280)

- **Published:** 2026-09-03
- **Type / source:** Paper · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors hold tasks fixed and instead grow the harness across tools, skills and agents over 17 multi-stage streams covering 802 tasks, 520 tools, 42 skills and 62 agents. They report harness-induced forgetting of up to 34.7 percent backward transfer on the agents axis with model parameters unchanged.

## 5. HarnessDev: Can LLMs Create and Evolve Their Own Agent Harness?

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01437)

- **Published:** 2026-09-01
- **Type / source:** Paper · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors test six creator models on harness creation across four domains and 2,207 held-out instances. They report that subsequent harness evolution yields unstable gains and only partial transfer, with outcomes strongly dependent on the model executing the generated harness.

## 6. WHALE: A Simple Recipe for Joint Harness-Weight Optimization

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00196)

- **Published:** 2026-08-31
- **Type / source:** Paper · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors alternate model-weight updates with executable harness search, reporting gains of 4.15 to 24.38 percentage points over the tested single-component and prompt-weight baselines across three domains with Qwen3.5-2B/4B agents. Code is released for reproducing the joint optimization recipe.

## 8. Context as an Environment: Programmatic Context Management for Long-Horizon Agents

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21690)

- **Published:** 2026-08-21
- **Type / source:** Paper · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors present Scroll, which exposes an append-only event log through a persistent Python environment so agents can retrieve and transform session state programmatically. With Qwen3.8-Max, they report 86.7 percent on LOCA_256K, 37.4 points above the best published long-horizon agent in their comparison.

## 9. AI4AI-Bench: Benchmarking LLM Agents in Algorithmic Design for Recursive Self-Improvement

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20318)

- **Published:** 2026-08-20
- **Type / source:** Paper · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors give agents four hours on one B300 to rewrite training algorithms across ten frozen repositories, then rerun submissions under hidden evaluation. Across 29 configurations, they report a mean score of 0.166 and a best score of 0.250, with most submissions leaving the learning procedure unchanged.

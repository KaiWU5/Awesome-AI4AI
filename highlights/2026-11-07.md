## 1. The components of agent memory

## 2. SwarmEvolve: Latent Agent Swarms for Evolutionary Discovery and Optimization

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08483)

- **Published:** 2026-11-05
- **Type / source:** Paper · Jiang, Qile et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors use latent-space agent swarms and KV-cache exchange for evolutionary program search. On 21 mathematical and scientific-computing problems, they report matching competing frameworks’ 60-minute best scores in a median 3–8 minutes.

## 3. EnzymeRSI: A dual-loop recursive self-improvement system for enzyme design

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06321)

- **Published:** 2026-11-04
- **Type / source:** Paper · Genoria AI Team et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors evolve both harness assets and enzyme-design algorithms. They report 10 of 32 non-pre-specified luciferase candidates with at least twice wild-type activity, plus prospective A3A and polymerase tests; physical-loop results are distinguished from development-set optimization.

## 4. SelfImprove-Bench: Evaluating Generalization in Self-Improving Agents

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02797)

- **Published:** 2026-11-02
- **Type / source:** Paper · Xu, Yang et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors introduce a reproducible RSI benchmark with a G0–G4 generalization ladder spanning exploration, planning and reasoning, and report that evolution gains do not consistently transfer across levels.

## 5. Exo: An Architecture for Self-Improving Systems at Runtime

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00463)

- **Published:** 2026-10-30
- **Type / source:** Paper · Krentsel et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors let an agent revise its harness during ongoing work behind a protected activation layer. Against a fixed-policy control, they report 35–61% lower held-out model cost on AppWorld and SREGym with comparable correctness.

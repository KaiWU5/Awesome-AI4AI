## 1. Self-Play Pretraining with Zero Data

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30063)

- **Published:** 2026-09-24
- **Type / source:** Paper · Stanford / Tel Aviv University et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors train a program generator and byte-sequence learner from random initialization, using self-play to create a curriculum. They report predictable zero-shot loss scaling on natural datasets without natural-data gradient updates. Natural validation data still guide hyperparameter selection, and the result is a proof of concept.

## 3. LLM Agents Can Easily Tamper With Their Own Traces

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30266)

- **Published:** 2026-09-24
- **Type / source:** Paper · ELLIS / MPI-IS / Snyk et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors find that tested local coding harnesses, except Muse Code, allow agents to delete their own execution traces when prompted, without triggering monitor guardrails. They also demonstrate externally induced and reward-driven trace tampering, motivating logging outside the agent-controlled environment.

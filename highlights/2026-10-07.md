## 7. SelfSearch: Reward-Free Search for Self-Improving Agents

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37968)

- **Published:** 2026-09-29
- **Type / source:** Paper · Jungwoo Yang / Injin Kong / Yohan Jo · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors search over agent modifications using prior self-improvement records without downstream reward signals during search.

## 1. RoboEvo2: Recursive Robot Self-Improvement via Harness–Policy Co-Evolution

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34675)

- **Published:** 2026-11-25
- **Type / source:** Paper · Liu et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors couple harness search with policy distillation, raising hybrid success from 30.4% to 90.5% on LIBERO-PRO without additional human demonstrations. On a real robot, the hybrid reaches 95% after training on 120 simulation-evolved demonstrations; the results do not imply data-free or unrestricted self-improvement.

## 2. TransHarness: From Benchmark Overfitting to Transferable Harness Evolution

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33757)

- **Published:** 2026-11-25
- **Type / source:** Paper · Xia et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors evolve reusable harness policies from 300 out-of-test-distribution trajectories and transfer them across tasks. With GPT-6 Astra, they report 76.4% on DeepSWE v1.1 and 95.5% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, gains of 2.4 and 5.6 points over strong baselines; mature harnesses still win on some settings.

## 3. GLM-5.4: Toward Generalized Coding with Our First Loop Transformer

[**Read source →**](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.4?lang=en)

- **Published:** 2026-11-23
- **Type / source:** Model release · Z.ai
- **Why it matters:** Z.ai introduces a 750B flagship that loops 28 shared layers twice, with per-token update gates and reused attention caches. Its controlled 30B ablation reports gains with about 10% extra training compute and 10–14% fewer output tokens. Chat and coding products launch first; API access and open weights remain planned.

## 4. ComputeWise: Measuring and Fixing GPU Waste by AI Research Agents

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31027)

- **Published:** 2026-11-23
- **Type / source:** Paper · Pakhomov et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors replay recorded training runs in a simulated-GPU benchmark, finding that only 52% of agent GPU time serves necessary work. Validating experiment plans removes over 80% of excess time-to-answer across five models. This measures avoidable workflow overhead in replay, rather than an 80% reduction in total real-world training cost.

## 5. Weight Coding: Evolving Web-Scale Recommenders through Direct Weight Edits with LLM Agents

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29613)

- **Published:** 2026-11-22
- **Type / source:** Paper · Hou et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors let agents inspect and directly edit retrieval-model weights without backpropagation. On a 147M-parameter image-search model, they report up to 15 points more recall@10 using about one quarter of full fine-tuning GPU hours; production A/B tests show 8.4–10.7% more saves per user from the edited retrieval source.

## 6. Language Model Harnesses can be Compositional Generalizers

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28298)

- **Published:** 2026-11-20
- **Type / source:** Paper · Zhang et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors train recursive harnesses on shorter and synthetic surrogate tasks, reporting generalization to tasks 2–64 times longer. In the same harness, RLM-27B raises an eight-task average from 46.1 to 58.5 with about half as many inference tokens; this does not establish lower wall-clock or training cost.

## 2. HERD: Harness-Evolving Recursive Distillation for Continual Learning

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31401)

- **Published:** 2026-10-22
- **Type / source:** Paper · Sun, Xun et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors introduce HERD, combining continual skill evolution and bounded residual memory with drift-constrained distillation into model parameters. On the 25-stage TRACE+17 stream under fixed memory and compute budgets, they report competitive new-task acquisition with the least forgetting among the compared methods.

## 5. Evolving Agent Interfaces

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28601)

## 7. Epi: Towards a Persistent, Self-Improving Agent for Autonomous Research

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24944)

- **Published:** 2026-10-18
- **Type / source:** Paper · Yue Huang et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors introduce Epi, combining persistent research history, hierarchical memory, branching and independent review across sessions under fixed verification. ## 10. Harness²: Recursive Agent Harnessing for an Open World

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14224)

- **Published:** 2026-10-09
- **Type / source:** Paper · Google Research
- **Why it matters:** Google Research introduces two connected loops that refine the current task harness and retain evidence-supported lessons for future tasks, with model weights fixed.
